Become a Client

NM36 Logistics is a B2B freight forwarder for the companies that make and supply India's exports, from furniture workshops and engineering manufacturers to merchandise vendors and trading houses.

Our vision is to become the most trusted logistics partner for manufacturers and B2B traders shipping from India, giving every factory, large or small, a reliable route to buyers on every continent.

Our mission is to move every consignment safely, on schedule and at a cost that keeps our clients competitive, through transparent pricing, proactive communication and complete compliance.

Our values are simple: all-in quotes with no hidden charges, honest timelines, cost discipline on every route and partnerships that grow from first samples to full-container programmes.

What types of cargo do you handle?

All general and industrial cargo: wooden furniture, electrical and electronics, mechanical parts and machinery, merchandise, textiles, home décor, auto components, industrial materials and building supplies. We do not handle food or perishable items.

Which countries do you ship to?

We ship from India to the USA, Australia, the UK, the UAE and Middle East, Europe and Canada through our destination partner network, and we can arrange custom lanes to almost any other country on request.

How does online slot booking work?

Pick a scheduled sailing from the slot board or start a custom booking, choose your freight mode, route and cargo, then select a factory pickup date and time window. Our team confirms the booking and your all-in rate, and the slot is held while we confirm.

Can you consolidate goods from several suppliers?

Yes. We collect from multiple Indian vendors, quality-check and store the goods at our consolidation facility, then ship everything together as one consignment under a single bill of lading.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. We file the shipping bill and handle Indian export clearance, HS code classification, certificates of origin and phytosanitary or fumigation certificates, and we arrange import clearance at destination through our partners.

Is cargo insurance available?

Yes. All-risk marine and air cargo insurance can be added to any consignment, with claims support from our team if anything goes wrong in transit.

Which Incoterms do you work with?

All of them: EXW, FCA, FOB, CFR, CIF, DAP, DPU and DDP. Whether your buyer collects ex-works or expects delivered duty paid, we price and run the shipment on the terms in your contract.

How quickly will I receive a quote?

Send us your cargo, route and ready date and our freight team replies with a clear, all-in rate, usually within one business day. Regular shippers can also get contracted rates and monthly slot allocation.
